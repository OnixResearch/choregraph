## Why

The immutable local candidate now has an operator-authorized Radicle RID and governed read-only acquisition paths. Consumers need exact publication evidence before source cutover.

Bind the existing candidate to its Radicle identity, parent-feature signed refs, independent replicas, and exact HTTPS acquisition without changing the candidate source revision.

## Non-goals

This change does not move the candidate source, grant consumer acceptance, prove indefinite availability, or widen Choregraph runtime authority.
